It is not too late

A focused week can change how you walk in. You do not need to learn everything. You need to fix the few things that will cost you the most.

Start with your school’s format and your weakest station type, then practise out loud under a timer.

When time is short, focus on

  • Your reasons for medicine
  • Your weakest station type
  • Timing and structure
  • Resetting after a bad station

Your 7-day plan

  1. Days 7 and 6

    Format and stories

    • Find your school’s station count and timings
    • Write six stories: motivation, teamwork, leadership, a mistake, a challenge, caring
    • Learn the six NHS values
  2. Days 5 and 4

    Structures out loud

    • Ethics with the four principles
    • Role play: ideas, concerns, expectations
    • Record two answers and watch them back
  3. Days 3 and 2

    Timed practice

    • A timed mock with honest feedback
    • Fix one thing per station
    • Read three health news stories
  4. Day 1

    Light and calm

    • Quick recap of your stories only
    • Plan route, clothes and ID
    • Early night

On the day

Before

Arrive settled

  • Aim to arrive early
  • Eat something and bring water
  • Read over your six stories once, then stop
Reading time

Plan before you speak

  • Pick the station type
  • Choose your structure
  • Note the one point you must make
Between stations

Reset

  • Breathe in for 4, hold for 4, out for 4
  • Leave the last station behind
  • Every station is marked separately

What not to do the night before

  • Memorising scripts→Know your ideas and examples, then speak naturally
  • Trying to learn everything→Fix the few things that cost you the most
  • Staying up late reading forums→Sleep is worth more than one more answer
  • Carrying a bad station→Reset. Read. Think. Start again
