Why KMMS needs its own practice

KMMS says its MMI is considerably different from most UK medical school interviews. It also says some previous applicants found normal school or commercial MMI preparation unhelpful for the KMMS process.

That does not mean you should skip preparation. It means you should prepare for the KMMS format, and not like a robot.

What we practise

KMMS has publicly listed these historical station types. We write our own practice stations around them.

01

Data handling

Read the information calmly and pull out what matters.

02

Problem analysis

  • What do I know?
  • What is the problem?
  • What information is missing?
  • What would I do next?
03

Situational judgement

  • Is anyone in danger?
  • What is my role?
  • Does someone senior need to know?
04

Role play

Do not act. Communicate.

Listen → Understand → Clarify → Respond

05

Task

Follow the brief, stay calm and talk through your thinking.

06

Values

Compassion, respect, dignity, responsibility and quality of care. Show the behaviour, not just the word.

07

Group station skills

Listen, invite quieter people in, build on ideas and help the group summarise.

What we do not do

  • No real interview questions.We only use public KMMS guidance and practice stations we write ourselves.
  • No scripts.KMMS looks for real communication, so we train skills and ideas, not memorised answers.
  • No promises of an offer.Nobody can promise one. We help you walk in prepared.
