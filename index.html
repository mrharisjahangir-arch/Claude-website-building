FAQ

Common questions

When should I start UCAT preparation?+

Most students see the best results starting focused preparation three to six months before their test date, though it's never too early to start building the underlying skills.

Do you offer 1-to-1 tutoring?+

Yes — 1-to-1 is available across UCAT, MMI/interview preparation, applications and GCSE/A-Level tutoring.

Do you offer group sessions?+

Yes, small group sessions are available alongside 1-to-1 — tell us your preference when you get in touch.

Do you help with medical school applications?+

Yes — university selection, personal statement support and overall application strategy.

Do you provide MMI mock interviews?+

Yes — realistic mock stations and traditional interview practice, with structured feedback afterwards.

Who are your tutors?+

Medical students and graduates from UK medical schools — see Meet Your Tutor above. The team is growing.

What subjects do you teach?+

GCSE Maths and Sciences, A-Level Chemistry, alongside all of our medicine-specific programmes.

How are sessions delivered?+

Sessions can run online or in person depending on your tutor and location — get in touch to find out what's available to you.

How much does tutoring cost?+

Pricing depends on the type and frequency of support you need. Get in touch and we'll talk you through the options.

Can parents book on behalf of students?+

Yes — our enquiry form includes an optional parent/guardian contact field.
