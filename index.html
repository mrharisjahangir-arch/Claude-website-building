The specialism

Kent and Medway runs its own interview. We prepare you for that one.

Most interview coaching treats every MMI as the same exercise. KMMS does not work that way. Its station format and what it rewards are specific to the school, and our tutors have sat it themselves rather than read about it.

If Kent and Medway is on your list, this is the difference between generic practice and practice that matches the day.
