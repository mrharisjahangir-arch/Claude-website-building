The MindMed method

A better way to prepare.

Effective preparation is not about doing more. It is about knowing where you are, understanding what you need to improve, and deliberately working towards it.

01 — ASSESS

Know your starting point

Understand where you are and identify your strengths and weaknesses.

02 — PLAN

03 — PREPARE

04 — PRACTISE
