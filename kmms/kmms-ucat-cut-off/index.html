KMMS UCAT cut-off 2027

What UCAT score do you need for KMMS? There is no confirmed KMMS UCAT cut-off for 2027 yet. Here is the previous threshold and how Kent and Medway Medical School uses the UCAT.

UCAT tutor in Canterbury →Back to the KMMS guide

Short answer

There is no confirmed KMMS UCAT cut-off for 2027 yet.

KMMS sets a threshold each year. In the 2025–26 cycle it was 2490 plus SJT Band 3 or better for Groups A and B. Do not treat that as the 2027 cut-off.

How KMMS uses the UCAT

You need to pass the threshold. But KMMS does not simply rank everyone from highest UCAT to lowest. Once you pass the threshold, another selection stage follows.

In short: the UCAT gets you through the gate. It does not win the whole application.
