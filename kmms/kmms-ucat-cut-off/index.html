KMMS UCAT cut-off 2027

What UCAT score do you need for KMMS? There is no confirmed KMMS UCAT cut-off for 2027 yet. Here is the previous threshold, how KMMS uses the UCAT and how to plan your choices around your score.

UCAT tutor in Canterbury →Back to the KMMS guide

Short answer

There is no confirmed KMMS UCAT cut-off for 2027 yet.

KMMS sets a threshold each year. In the 2025–26 cycle it was 2490 plus SJT Band 3 or better for Groups A and B. Do not treat that as the 2027 cut-off.

How KMMS uses the UCAT

You need to pass the threshold. But KMMS does not simply rank everyone from highest UCAT to lowest. Once you pass the threshold, another selection stage follows, where your grades are looked at in the context of your school.

In short: the UCAT gets you through the gate. It does not win the whole application.

UCAT scoring in plain English

Three scored subtests

Verbal Reasoning, Decision Making and Quantitative Reasoning are each scored 300 to 900, giving a total from 900 to 2700.

Situational Judgement

Reported separately, as Band 1 to 4. Band 1 is the highest.

Old scores

Scores from the previous /3600 system should not be compared directly with current scores.
