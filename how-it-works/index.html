How to get the most from your first session

Bring your deadlines

Exam dates, UCAT date, UCAS deadline or interview dates. They shape the plan.

Bring your work

A recent test, a draft statement or your UCAT scores so far.

Bring your questions

Write down what worries you most. That is usually the right place to start.
