What is in the UCAT?

The UCAT has three scored subtests, Verbal Reasoning, Decision Making and Quantitative Reasoning, which combine into a score from 900 to 2700. Situational Judgement is reported separately as Band 1 to 4.

Each medical school uses the UCAT differently. Some weigh your score heavily. Others set a threshold, then look at other things. KMMS, for example, sets a threshold and then looks at your grades in the context of your school.
